// Meet The Designer

Julian Vance

Founded on the core philosophy of architectural functionalism, Soul Architect explores the raw intersection between structural design and modern garment construction. Every piece is meticulously stripped of unnecessary ornamentation to highlight clean form, material weight, and uncompromised utility. Operating out of an industrial studio space, Julian Vance approaches fashion as an extension of spatial discipline, creating functional artifacts engineered to endure.

See More +

By utilizing heavy-gauge textiles and industrial leather, Julian Vance focuses on creating objects that interact directly with spatial geometry. The collection serves as an ongoing investigation into how clean lines and raw materials shape personal identity. Each silhouette is developed through rigorous pattern prototyping, prioritizing silhouette retention and structural integrity over seasonal trends. The studio operates with a brutalist design framework, sourcing raw materials that age naturally and reveal their underlying character over time. Through a commitment to slow manufacturing and deliberate aesthetic restraint, every release stands as a permanent fixture in an evolving architectural wardrobe.
