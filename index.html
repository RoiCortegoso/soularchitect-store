// Meet The Designer

Antia Cortegoso

I believe fashion is a way to express how you feel and what you believe. Through art, you can heal scars, give voice to different topics, and, of course, show who you are.

See More +

My work explores the relationship between fashion, identity, and emotion. I am interested in how garments can become a reflection of what happens within us, transforming intangible feelings, personal experiences, and social topics into something physical and visible. Through experimentation with silhouettes, construction, materials, and textile manipulation, I create pieces that invite the viewer to look beyond the surface and connect with the story behind each design. For me, fashion is not only about what we wear, but also about what we choose to communicate through it.
